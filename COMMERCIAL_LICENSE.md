## 1. Who needs a commercial license ?

You need a commercial license if **any of the following is true** for your usage of ELY :

| Situation | Commercial license required ? |
|---|---|
| You use ELY at home for yourself or your family | ❌ No (personal use) |
| You use ELY as a student or for self-learning | ❌ No (educational use) |
| You install ELY on a server you own and use it alone for your daily tasks | ❌ No (personal use) |
| You evaluate ELY in a corporate POC for less than 30 days | ❌ No (evaluation use) |
| **You install ELY for your colleagues / employees to use** | ✅ **Yes** |
| **You host ELY and offer it as a service to your customers** | ✅ **Yes** |
| **You integrate ELY into a product you sell or monetize** | ✅ **Yes** |
| **You use ELY internally at your company beyond 30 days of evaluation** | ✅ **Yes** |
| **You sell consulting / setup / managed services around ELY** | ✅ **Yes** |
| **You use the ELY name or branding in a paid offering** | ✅ **Yes** (also see [TRADEMARK.md](./TRADEMARK.md)) |

**Rule of thumb** : if any money changes hands directly or indirectly because of ELY, OR if a third party uses ELY through you (employees, clients, friends-and-family that you "set up"), you need a commercial license.

## 6. FAQ

**Q : I'm a non-profit, do I get a discount ?**
A : Yes. 50% off any tier, no questions asked. Just mention your non-profit name & website in the email.

**Q : I'm a student / open-source contributor, can I use ELY commercially in my own indie project ?**
A : Drop us a line — we frequently issue free commercial licenses to indie devs and OSS contributors. We just want to know who you are.

**Q : I want to integrate ELY into my SaaS, what do I do ?**
A : You need at minimum a **Business** tier. The "white-label" option (your branding, no ELY name visible) is available at Enterprise tier or as a one-time fee on Business tier — let's talk.

**Q : What if I'm already using ELY commercially without a license ?**
A : Email us **before** we find out. We always offer a fair retroactive arrangement to people who self-disclose. We're far less generous when we have to chase you.

**Q : Can I get a perpetual license instead of subscription ?**
A : Yes, available at Enterprise tier as a one-time fee = 36× monthly price + 1 year of updates included. After that, support / updates are an annual maintenance fee (~20% of the perpetual price).

**Q : What if you discontinue the project ?**
A : All paying customers get a perpetual license to the **last shipped version** of their tier, plus the source code (already public anyway), plus the right to fork under the ELY name (unique grant). You won't be stranded.

**Q : Can I cancel anytime ?**
A : Yes, no commitment beyond the current billing period. Your installation keeps working under your existing personal/educational rights — you just lose the commercial use grant.
